The Problem

The leadership gap holding India's startups back

India produces extraordinary founders. But between the scrappy early days and the scale-up phase sits a dangerous gap — the moment when a company needs experienced C-suite leadership but can't yet justify the cost of full-time CXOs.

That gap costs companies years of growth, misdirected strategy, and talent that leaves without strong leaders above them. FxN was built to close it.

Months wasted on the wrong hires

Traditional executive search takes 4–6 months. By then, the market window has often closed.

CXO salaries that don't fit the stage

A full-time CMO or CFO at ₹1Cr+ per year is out of reach for most growth-stage companies.

Mismatched expertise for the challenge

Even when leaders are hired, they rarely have exactly the right expertise for the specific challenge at hand.

Common Questions

FAQs

How is the Pod structured?

The GTM Pod runs for 12 weeks with one live session per week. Each session is practitioner-led and interactive, built around your real company data. You'll leave each session with clear outputs and actions — not just notes. Between sessions, you have async access to practitioners and fellow founders in a private pod group.

Is my company data kept private?

Yes. All company data shared within the cohort is kept strictly confidential. You'll share within a small, vetted group of non-competing founders and their GTM leads. Practitioners sign NDAs and all participants agree to a confidentiality commitment at the start of the cohort.

How is this different from generic GTM courses?

Generic GTM courses give you frameworks. The FxN GTM Pod gives you working playbooks built on your own pipeline data, reviewed by practicing operators who've scaled real B2B companies. There are no pre-recorded videos and no generic templates — everything is built live with your actual context.

What if I'm pre-revenue or very early stage?

The GTM Pod is designed for funded B2B startups or companies with $1M+ revenue that already have some GTM motion. If you're pre-revenue, we recommend speaking with our team to find the right FxN engagement for your stage.

Who leads the sessions?

Every session is led by a practicing operator — not a career educator or course creator. The seven core practitioners are all active fractional CXOs within the FxN network, with 20–30+ years of experience each. Guest practitioners with specialist expertise join select sessions across the cohort.

What happens after the 12 weeks?

You graduate with a complete, executable GTM Playbook that's ready to run from day one. For companies that need ongoing fractional leadership support beyond the cohort, FxN can match you with the right CXO from our partner network.
